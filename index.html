before you book

Questions people actually ask.

What is hypnotherapy, really?

It's a guided conversation combined with deep relaxation and focused attention. You stay aware throughout. It helps you explore what's going on beneath the surface and work with it.

Will I lose control?

No. You stay in control the whole time. It's in our tagline: explore, understand, control. You can stop or speak at any point.

What happens in my first session?

You and your therapist talk through what brought you here and what you'd like to change. Then you decide together how to work on it. You don't need to prepare anything.

Online or in clinic: which is better?

Both work. Online is the easiest first step and the most flexible. In clinic is face to face in Jakarta. Home visits are there if travelling is hard. Choose whichever helps you feel most at ease.

Is what I share kept private?

Yes. Sessions are one to one, in a safe and confidential setting.

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on what you're working on. Your therapist will talk this through with you, and packages are available if you'd like several sessions.
